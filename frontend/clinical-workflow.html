From patient words to verified chart

The clinical desk is where preparation becomes verification.

Every visit moves through real, persisted states. No step is a screen animation — each transition writes to the database, creates an audit entry, and updates the patient’s dashboard only when the backend confirms it.

1
Queue with real metrics
Pending Reviews, In Intake, Active Emergencies, Verified Today — all counted from live backend queries, never hardcoded.
2
Search & filter
Find by patient name, ID, visit ID, status. Filters for Awaiting Doctor, In Intake, Emergency, Verified.
3
Open Case → Workspace
Tabs: Overview (status, docs, conflicts), AI Summary (source-labelled), History (structured comparison), Documents (real viewer), Intake (raw Q&A), Timeline (audit-backed events).
4
Document viewer — real files
PDF rendered inline via authenticated /view, images with zoom, fullscreen, close, download. 403 if not authorized. No filename-only modal.
5
Review & correct
Edit narrative or structured history — saving creates a new version, not an overwrite. Every version is kept.
6
Verify or reject
Doctor identity, visit, timestamp and status are persisted. Patient dashboard moves only after backend confirmation. Emergency desk: Open → Acknowledge → Resolve, all persisted.

AI prepares structured context. It does not diagnose or prescribe. A registered practitioner must verify.
