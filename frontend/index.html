The patient journey

Nine steps, from a first word to a verified case.

1

Identity & consent

Patient ID, language, purpose-limited consent — stored per visit, before any question.

2

Main problem

Six complaint pathways — fever to “other”. A conversation, never a diagnosis menu.

3

Adaptive interview

Symptom details, duration, red flags — adaptive to what the patient has already said.

4

Answer by voice or text

Every question typed, tapped or spoken. Transcripts stay in the patient’s language.

5

Health history & AYUSH

Medical, surgical, family history, medications, allergies. AYUSH is optional; “not sure” is a first-class answer.

6

Documents & OCR

Prescriptions, labs, imaging (PDF/JPG/PNG). Entities extracted with confidence; low-confidence flagged for review.

7

AI structures it

A versioned structured context — every item labelled patient-reported, document-extracted or AI-organized.

8

Patient confirms or corrects

The draft is read back. Corrections navigate to the right question. Confirmation is stored in the database.

9

Doctor verifies

Verification is a real state change — the patient’s dashboard moves only when the backend confirms it.

Returning-visit intelligence

If your previous visit is structurally related (same pathway, shared medications or allergies — never plain text matching), you can review it and answer only what may have changed.

Capabilities

Doctor desk with real verification

Queue with search and filters, full patient workspace, document viewer, versioned summaries, and verification written to the database.

Doctor sign in

Emergency safety net

Fixed red-flag rules run in the backend. Patient-triggered and rule-triggered alerts reach the desk in real time, with acknowledge/resolve states and audit entries.

Safety model

Four languages, honestly

English, Hindi, Tamil and Bengali. Voice recognition, interview text and buttons follow the chosen language; the transcript is never silently translated.

How it works

A working prototype: the AI provider, OCR and speech services are deterministic demo implementations behind clean interfaces. Records, permissions, workflows and audit are real.
