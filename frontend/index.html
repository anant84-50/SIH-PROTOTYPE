Patient Journey

How a Patient Visit Moves from Intake to Verified Case

Nine steps, from a first word to a verified case. Each step stores real data, not a mock.

1

Identity & consent

Patient ID, language, purpose-limited consent — stored per visit, before any question.

2

Main problem

Six complaint pathways — fever to “other”. A conversation, never a diagnosis menu.

3

Adaptive interview

Symptom details, duration, red flags — adaptive to what the patient has already said.

4

Answer by voice or text

Every question typed, tapped or spoken. Transcripts stay in the patient’s language.

5

Health history & AYUSH

Medical, surgical, family history, medications, allergies. AYUSH is optional; “not sure” is a first-class answer.

6

Documents & OCR

Prescriptions, labs, imaging (PDF/JPG/PNG). Entities extracted with confidence; low-confidence flagged for review.

7

AI structures it

A versioned structured context — every item labelled patient-reported, document-extracted or AI-organized.

8

Patient confirms or corrects

The draft is read back. Corrections navigate to the right question. Confirmation is stored in the database.

9

Doctor verifies

Verification is a real state change — the patient’s dashboard moves only when the backend confirms it.

◉
Voice + Text Intake

Patient intake that stays in your language

Adaptive questions, quick-tap answers, voice with live feedback. The transcript lands in the answer box for review — never silently translated.

◫ Document & OCR

AI + documents become source-linked context

PDF/JPG/PNG uploaded, OCR queued in backend, entities extracted with confidence. Low-confidence stays NEEDS_REVIEW until a human closes it.

✚
✓ Verified
Doctor review

Doctor reviews with sources attached

Queue, workspace, versioned summaries, real document viewer, corrections and verification — all persisted with audit.

How it helps

Less time hunting history, more time with the patient

  • Returning-visit intelligence — structurally related visits surface, not text match.
  • Patient speaks in their language, transcript stays there.
  • Doctor gets a source-labelled draft before the consultation.
