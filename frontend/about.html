Ministry of Ayush · All India Institute of Ayurveda

A pre-clinical desk that prepares the chart — not the prescription.

Preclinic IQ AI is patient case-taking software. It gathers voice, text, touch answers and medical files, then hands a source-linked history to the doctor. AYUSH fields (prakriti, nidana, ahara, vihara) sit beside modern clinical history. The model never diagnoses or writes treatment.

Three desks

Patient · Doctor · Admin

Patients tell the story. Doctors verify. NexusCare administration watches safety, users and the full longitudinal record.

What is stored

One patient history

Every visit, interview answer, AYUSH note, uploaded prescription or lab, conflict and emergency is written to the patient timeline — visible later to that patient, authorised doctors, and admin.

Safety

Emergency is not an LLM

Red-flag rules run in Python. A dedicated Emergency control also alerts doctor and admin at once so a human can act.
