The journey of one visit

Nine steps, from a first word to a verified case.

1
Identity & consent
The patient signs in with their own Patient ID and gives purpose-limited consent for AI-assisted intake before any question is asked. The consent is stored per visit.
2
Adaptive interview
Six complaint pathways (fever, headache, and five more) drive an adaptive question bank — symptom details, duration, red flags. A pathway is a conversation, never a diagnosis menu.
3
Voice or text
Every question can be answered by voice (browser speech recognition), typed, or tapped from options. The interview and buttons follow the chosen language — English, Hindi, Tamil or Bengali.
4
Health history & AYUSH
Past conditions, surgeries, family history, current medications and allergies. AYUSH context — prakriti, vikriti, ahara, vihara, nidana — is optional, and “not sure” is a first-class answer.
5
Documents & OCR
Prescriptions, lab reports and imaging are uploaded (PDF/JPG/PNG). A backend extractor pulls structured entities with confidence; low-confidence items are flagged for human review, not silently accepted.
6
AI structuring
The AI provider assembles a versioned structured context: presenting concern, symptoms & timeline, medical/surgical/family history, medications, allergies, AYUSH, document-derived data — each item labelled patient-reported, document-extracted or AI-organized.
7
Patient confirmation
The patient reads the draft, can correct any information (the system navigates back to the relevant question) and confirms it. Confirmation — who, when, for which visit — is stored in the database.
8
Doctor review
The doctor opens the case: overview, AI summary, history comparison, documents, raw intake and timeline. They may edit supported structured fields, saving a new review version.
9
Doctor verification
Verification (or rejection) is a real state change: doctor identity, timestamp, visit and status are persisted, a timeline event is recorded, and the patient’s dashboard updates only once the backend confirms it.

AI prepares structured context. It does not diagnose or prescribe. A registered practitioner must verify.

Patients

Your dashboard shows the visit pipeline step by step — each step completes only when the backend confirms it. Your history, documents, notifications and an emergency assistance flow are all real, persisted records.

Doctors

The clinical desk has a searchable queue with filters, a full patient workspace, working document viewers, and verification that writes to the database. Nothing is verified until the doctor says so.

Administration

The existing admin console is unchanged: patients, visits, documents, emergency inbox and the audit trail of every clinical action.

This is not an ambulance service. If someone is collapsing, call local emergency numbers as well as alerting the desk.

Working process of intake → summary → doctor verify is unchanged.
