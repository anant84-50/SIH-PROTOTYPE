Usage guide

From first word to a verified chart — without changing clinical judgement.

1. Patient

  1. Sign in with Patient ID (example PCI-2026-000001).
  2. Pick a language — English, Hindi, Tamil or Bengali. The interview follows it.
  3. Give consent, choose a complaint pathway (not a diagnosis).
  4. Answer by voice, text or touch. Upload prescriptions or labs.
  5. Confirm the AI draft. History is saved to your record.
  6. Use the red Emergency control if you need the doctor and admin immediately.

2. Doctor

  1. Sign in as DOC-001.
  2. Open the intake queue or any saved patient record.
  3. Read history, AYUSH, timeline and uploaded files (Open file).
  4. Resolve conflicts. Verify or reject the summary — a new version is kept.
  5. Acknowledge emergency alerts and act. The system does not prescribe.

3. Admin (NexusCare)

  1. Sign in with Admin ID NexusCare and password 2525.
  2. See every patient, visit count, uploaded documents and the audit trail.
  3. Open a patient to read the same saved history the doctor sees.
  4. Watch the emergency inbox and system health.

This is not an ambulance service. If someone is collapsing, call local emergency numbers as well as alerting the desk.

Working process of intake → summary → doctor verify is unchanged.
